FEATURED PROJECTS

⭐ Flagship Project

Durban Beach Feeding Scheme

T&T Marine has been instrumental in supplying, commissioning, and managing the pumping of sand along the Durban beachfront to replenish sand removed by wave action. This ongoing project is critical for maintaining the iconic beaches of Durban and protecting coastal infrastructure.

Project Scope:

  • Sand pumping system design
  • Equipment installation
  • Ongoing operation management
  • Beach profile maintenance

Key Achievements:

  • Sustained beach replenishment
  • Coastal infrastructure protection
  • Minimal environmental impact
  • Continuous operation capability

Koeberg Power Station (Eskom)

Our team conducted specialized dredging operations at the main intake basin of the Koeberg Nuclear Plant. This critical project required precision, stringent safety protocols, and minimal disruption to the power station's operations.

Project Scope:

  • Intake basin dredging
  • Sediment removal and disposal
  • Bathymetric surveys
  • Environmental monitoring

Key Achievements:

  • Zero safety incidents
  • No operational disruption
  • Completed ahead of schedule
  • Improved water flow efficiency

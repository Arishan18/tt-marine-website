Our Foundation

COMPANY OVERVIEW

T&T Marine is South Africa's leading dredging and marine contractor, specializing primarily in dredging as well as harbour construction and land reclamation. Established with a vision to provide exceptional marine services, we have grown to become a trusted name in the industry.

Our main business segment is Marine Civils, where we specialize in dredging, harbour construction and earth moving, including the construction and maintenance of ports and waterways, land reclamation, coastal defense and river rehabilitation.

As the leading dredging contractor in South Africa, we also have a marine hydrographic surveying division that works with the dredging operation using bathymetric surveys to ensure that design levels are attained.

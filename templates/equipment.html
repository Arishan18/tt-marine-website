DREDGING EQUIPMENT

High Capacity

CSD 350

Our Cutting Suction Dredger (CSD) 350 is a powerful dredging solution capable of handling a wide range of materials. With its advanced cutting mechanism, it efficiently removes sediment and deposits from various underwater environments.

Key Features:

  • Powerful cutting mechanism
  • High-capacity pumping system
  • Versatile operational capabilities
  • Modular design for easy transport

Technical Specifications:

  • Dredging depth: up to 9m
  • Production capacity: 350m³/h
  • Total installed power: 450 kW
  • Pipeline diameter: 350mm

Cutter Head for Up to 9 MPA Material

Our specialized cutter head is designed to handle extremely tough materials up to 9 MPA in hardness. This robust attachment enables efficient dredging operations in challenging conditions where conventional equipment would struggle.

Key Features:

  • Hardened steel construction
  • Optimized cutting pattern
  • Replaceable cutting teeth
  • Compatible with CSD 350

Technical Specifications:

  • Diameter: 800mm
  • Material capacity: up to 9 MPA
  • Rotation speed: 35 RPM
  • Power requirement: 120 kW

Excavator Fleet

EXCAVATORS

⭐ Unique in SA

Amphibious CAT 320 DL Excavator on Floating Track Pontoons

Our amphibious excavator is a unique piece of equipment in South Africa, designed to operate effectively in both water and land environments. The floating track pontoons allow the machine to navigate through swampy areas, shallow water, and soft terrain that would be inaccessible to conventional excavators.

Key Features:

  • Amphibious capability
  • Self-propelled in water
  • Low ground pressure
  • Customizable for specific sites

Technical Specifications:

  • Base machine: CAT 320 DL
  • Operating weight: 25 tons
  • Max digging depth: 6.7m
  • Max reach: 9.9m

CAT 320 Long Reach Excavator

Our CAT 320 Long Reach Excavator is specially designed for extended reach applications, allowing for operations at greater distances and depths than standard excavators. This capability is particularly valuable for dredging operations, river works, and projects requiring extended reach while maintaining stability.

Key Features:

  • Extended boom and arm
  • Enhanced stability system
  • Versatile attachment options
  • Precise hydraulic control

Technical Specifications:

  • Max reach: 15.5m
  • Max digging depth: 12m
  • Operating weight: 22 tons
  • Engine power: 123 kW

Survey Technology

SURVEY EQUIPMENT

High Precision

Bathymetric Survey Systems

Our advanced bathymetric survey systems provide detailed mapping of underwater topography, ensuring accurate data for project planning and verification. These systems are essential for dredging operations, allowing us to precisely determine volumes and monitor progress.

Key Features:

  • High-resolution depth measurements
  • GPS integration
  • Real-time data processing
  • Comprehensive mapping capabilities

Applications:

  • Pre-dredging surveys
  • Progress monitoring
  • Final verification
  • Volume calculations

RTK GPS Base Stations and Moving Base Applications

Our Real-Time Kinematic (RTK) GPS systems provide centimeter-level positioning accuracy for precise equipment guidance and survey operations. These systems are crucial for maintaining exact positioning during dredging, excavation, and construction activities.

Key Features:

  • Centimeter-level accuracy
  • Real-time position corrections
  • Multiple base station capability
  • Integrated data management

Applications:

  • Equipment positioning
  • Dredging guidance
  • Survey operations
  • Site mapping and planning

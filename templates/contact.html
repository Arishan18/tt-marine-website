FAQ

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What areas do you service?

T&T Marine operates throughout South Africa and neighboring countries, including Namibia and Swaziland. We have completed projects in various locations and are equipped to mobilize our resources to meet your project needs across the Southern African region.

What types of projects do you handle?

We handle a wide range of marine and dredging projects, including harbor construction, land reclamation, coastal defense, river rehabilitation, and specialized dredging operations. Our projects range from small-scale operations to large industrial undertakings for major clients like Eskom and mining companies.

Do you offer equipment rental?

Yes, we offer equipment rental services, including generator hire and specialized marine equipment such as our unique amphibious excavators. Contact us with your specific requirements, and we'll provide you with suitable options and competitive rates.

How do I request a quote for my project?

You can request a quote by calling our office directly at +27 82 567 3669, contacting our project manager Lungi Mchunu at +27 71 689 2747, or sending us an email with your project details. Our team will review your requirements and provide you with a comprehensive quote based on your specific needs.

What environmental considerations do you take into account?

Environmental sustainability is a core value at T&T Marine. We comply with all relevant environmental regulations and implement best practices to minimize our ecological footprint. Our approach includes careful planning, use of environmentally friendly techniques, thorough environmental impact assessments, and comprehensive post-project restoration where applicable.

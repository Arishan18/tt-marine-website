Vibrocoring

Our specialized equipment for vibrocoring services allows for efficient extraction of core samples from underwater sediments, providing valuable data for project planning and execution.

Vibrocoring is an essential technique for geotechnical investigations in marine environments, helping to understand subsurface conditions and inform engineering decisions.

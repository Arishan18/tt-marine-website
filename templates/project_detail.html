Project Overview

The Durban Beach Feeding Scheme is a critical environmental and infrastructure project aimed at maintaining the iconic beaches of Durban. T&T Marine has been responsible for supplying, commissioning, and managing the pumping of sand along the Durban beachfront to replenish sand removed by wave action.

This ongoing project plays a vital role in preserving the coastal environment, protecting beachfront infrastructure, and maintaining the recreational and tourism value of Durban's beaches. Our comprehensive approach ensures continuous sand replenishment even during challenging weather conditions.

The project involves complex technical considerations, including pipeline design, pump station configuration, and monitoring systems to ensure optimal sand distribution along the beachfront. Our expertise in marine operations and dredging has been instrumental in the success of this long-term project.

Project Details

Scope of Work

  • Design and Installation:

    Design, supply, and installation of sand pumping systems along the Durban beachfront.

  • Continuous Operation:

    Ongoing management of the sand pumping operation to ensure consistent beach replenishment.

  • Monitoring:

    Regular monitoring of sand distribution, beach profiles, and system performance.

  • Maintenance:

    Regular maintenance of pumping equipment, pipelines, and associated infrastructure.

  • Environmental Management:

    Implementation of environmental monitoring and management practices to minimize ecological impact.

Project Highlights

  • Continuous Operation:

    Maintained uninterrupted sand pumping operations despite challenging weather conditions and technical challenges.

  • Beach Preservation:

    Successfully maintained the beach profile and width, preserving the recreational and tourism value of Durban's beaches.

  • Infrastructure Protection:

    Protected valuable beachfront infrastructure from erosion and wave damage through effective sand management.

  • Minimal Environmental Impact:

    Implemented environmentally sensitive practices to minimize disturbance to marine ecosystems and coastal processes.

  • Technical Innovation:

    Developed innovative solutions to optimize sand distribution and system efficiency throughout the project duration.

Project Overview

The Koeberg Nuclear Power Station project involved specialized dredging operations at the main intake basin of South Africa's only nuclear power plant. This critical infrastructure project required exceptional precision, stringent safety protocols, and minimal disruption to the power station's operations.

T&T Marine was selected for this high-profile project due to our expertise in specialized dredging operations and our proven track record of successful projects in sensitive environments. The project's primary objective was to restore the intake basin to its original design specifications, ensuring optimal water flow for the power station's cooling systems.

The project presented unique challenges due to the critical nature of the facility and the strict regulatory requirements associated with operations at a nuclear power plant. Our team implemented comprehensive safety and quality control measures throughout the project to ensure full compliance with all requirements while delivering exceptional results.

Project Details

Scope of Work

  • Pre-Dredging Survey:

    Comprehensive bathymetric survey to assess sediment accumulation and establish baseline conditions.

  • Dredging Operations:

    Precise dredging of the main intake basin to restore original design specifications and ensure optimal water flow.

  • Sediment Management:

    Responsible handling and disposal of dredged materials in accordance with environmental regulations.

  • Environmental Monitoring:

    Continuous monitoring of water quality and environmental parameters during dredging operations.

  • Post-Dredging Survey:

    Final bathymetric survey to verify achievement of design specifications and document completed work.

Project Achievements

  • Safety Excellence:

    Completed the entire project with zero safety incidents, despite the high-risk environment of a nuclear facility.

  • Operational Continuity:

    Maintained uninterrupted power station operations throughout the dredging project.

  • Environmental Compliance:

    Met or exceeded all environmental requirements and maintained excellent water quality throughout the project.

  • Ahead of Schedule:

    Completed the project ahead of the planned timeline, minimizing the operational impact on the power station.

  • Performance Improvement:

    Enhanced cooling water flow efficiency by restoring the intake basin to optimal design specifications.

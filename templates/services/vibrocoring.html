State-of-the-Art Sediment Sampling

Vibrocoring is a revolutionary sediment sampling technology that uses high-frequency, low-amplitude vibrations to obtain undisturbed cores of unconsolidated sediments. The vibrations liquify surrounding sediments, dramatically reducing friction and enabling precise penetration with minimal sample disturbance.

Unlike traditional coring methods such as gravity cores or box cores, vibrocoring achieves superior sample recovery rates with near-zero core compression, providing accurate true-depth samples essential for environmental compliance and geological assessment projects.

How It Works

VIBROCORING PROCESS

1

Positioning

Mobile barge is positioned at the designated sampling location using GPS coordinates and outboard motor for precise placement.

2

Deployment

Core tube with liner and steel nose piece is lowered to the sediment surface using the winch system.

3

Vibration

High-frequency vibrations liquify surrounding sediments, reducing friction and enabling smooth penetration to target depth.

4

Recovery

Core catcher and check valve retain sediments during extraction, ensuring high-quality undisturbed sample recovery.

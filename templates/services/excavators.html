CAT 320 LONG & SHORT REACH

⭐ Amphibious Capable

CAT 320 Long Reach Excavator

Our CAT 320 Long Reach excavators are specifically configured for marine applications with extended reach capabilities up to 32+ feet. These versatile machines can be equipped with amphibious pontoons for operations in wetlands, shallow water, and challenging coastal environments.

Technical Specifications:

  • Operating Weight: 25 tons
  • Max Reach: 32+ feet (9.9m)
  • Max Dig Depth: 20+ feet (6.7m)
  • Bucket Capacity: 0.6-0.78 m³

Key Features:

  • C7.1 Twin Turbo Engine
  • Amphibious Pontoon Compatible
  • Hydraulic Dredge Pump Support
  • Advanced Hydraulic Systems

Marine Applications:

Channel dredging • Harbor construction • Coastal defense • Beach nourishment • Environmental restoration • Wetland operations • Pipeline installation

CAT 320 Short Reach Excavator

The CAT 320 Short Reach configuration provides enhanced digging force and precision for close-range marine operations. Ideal for precise excavation work in confined spaces, loading operations, and detailed construction tasks.

Technical Specifications:

  • Operating Weight: 22-25 tons
  • Max Reach: 28 feet (8.5m)
  • Max Dig Depth: 18 feet (5.5m)
  • Bucket Capacity: 0.8-1.2 m³

Key Features:

  • Enhanced Digging Force
  • Precision Control Systems
  • Multi-Attachment Compatible
  • Superior Stability

Precision Applications:

Foundation work • Precise excavation • Loading operations • Utility installation • Breakwater construction • Marina development • Port maintenance

HITACHI ZX800 Long & Short Reach

The HITACHI ZX800 represents the pinnacle of heavy-duty excavation capability in our fleet. With exceptional reach configurations up to 26 meters and operating weights around 80 tons, this powerhouse tackles the largest marine construction projects with unmatched efficiency.

Technical Specifications:

  • Operating Weight: 78-82 tons
  • Long Reach: 26m (85+ feet)
  • Standard Reach: 27'1" boom / 11'10" stick
  • Bucket Capacity: 1.0-4.5 m³

Key Features:

  • High-Power Engine (450+ HP)
  • Extended Long Reach Arms
  • Heavy-Duty Construction
  • Superior Digging Force

Heavy-Duty Applications:

Large-scale dredging • Deep channel excavation • Major harbor construction • Breakwater installation • Land reclamation • Mining operations • Industrial demolition

HITACHI ZX850 Long & Short Reach

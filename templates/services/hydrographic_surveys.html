Single Beam Odom CV100 Echo Sounder System

Our state-of-the-art Teledyne Odom CV100 single beam echo sounder represents the pinnacle of depth measurement technology. This digital system eliminates traditional paper records in favor of precise PC-based data acquisition, delivering exceptional accuracy and reliability for bathymetric surveys.

Technical Specifications:

  • Depth Range: 0.3m to 600m
  • Accuracy: ±0.01m + 0.1% depth

  • Real-time data processing

Dual Frequency Transducer

Advanced transducer system offering both high and low frequency capabilities for optimal performance across different water depths and bottom conditions.

  • High frequency: 100-750kHz
  • Low frequency: 24-50kHz
  • Automatic selection

Motion compensation system that corrects depth measurements for vessel heave, pitch, and roll to ensure accurate bathymetric data in rough sea conditions.

  • Real-time correction
  • TSS1 compatible
  • Wave effect elimination

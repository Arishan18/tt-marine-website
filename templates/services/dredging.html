OUR DREDGING METHODS

High Capacity

Cutting Suction Dredgers

Our cutting suction dredgers are powerful machines designed to efficiently remove sediment, sand, gravel, and even harder materials from underwater environments. The cutter head breaks up the material, which is then pumped through a pipeline to the designated discharge area.

Key Features:

  • High production capacity
  • Suitable for various material types
  • Precise depth control
  • Continuous operation

Ideal Applications:

  • Harbor maintenance
  • Channel deepening
  • Land reclamation
  • Mining operations

Auger Heads

Auger head dredging is particularly effective for removing fine-grained, soft sediments in confined spaces. The rotating auger loosens and directs material toward the suction intake, ensuring efficient material removal with minimal turbidity.

Key Features:

  • Precise material removal
  • Low turbidity operation
  • Suitable for confined spaces
  • Efficient for soft sediments

Ideal Applications:

  • Marina maintenance
  • Environmental remediation
  • Confined area dredging
  • Silt removal

Excavating

Our excavator dredging operations utilize specialized long-reach or amphibious excavators to remove material directly from underwater environments. This method provides exceptional control and is particularly effective for projects requiring selective material removal or when dealing with debris-laden sediments.

Key Features:

  • Precise material extraction
  • Ability to handle debris
  • Selective dredging capability
  • Versatile positioning options

Ideal Applications:

  • Shoreline restoration
  • Debris-laden areas
  • Confined spaces
  • Hard material removal

DOP Pump Dredging

Our DOP (Dredging On Pontoon) pump technology offers versatile dredging solutions through submersible pumps that can be deployed in various configurations. This approach provides exceptional flexibility and is ideal for projects with limited access or unique operational requirements.

Key Features:

  • Highly versatile deployment
  • Compact design
  • Multiple attachment options
  • Suitable for remote locations

Ideal Applications:

  • Limited access areas
  • Industrial applications
  • Tailings pond management
  • Environmental cleanup
